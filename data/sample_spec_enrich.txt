ENRICH — Solar PV Plant Design Basis
Customer: Enrich  (Solar-Design-Configurator production customer)
Project: Enrich Utility-Scale PV — Block-01
Location: Khavda, Gujarat, India (latitude 23.85 N)
Mounting structure: MMS Table (32.2920 x 4.5760), C-section piling (12 piles per table)
Electrical hierarchy: Modules -> Strings -> SCB -> Inverter -> ASB -> LT Panel -> Transformer

Design Requirements

1.  The block shall have a target DC capacity of 5 MWp.
2.  Mounting shall be fixed-tilt at a tilt angle of 24 degrees.
3.  The module shall be a Waaree 550 Wp mono-PERC panel (Voc 49.9 V, Vmp 41.5 V,
    Isc 13.97 A, Imp 13.26 A, temperature coefficient of Voc -0.28 %/degC,
    dimensions 2.279 m x 1.134 m).
4.  The string open-circuit voltage at the minimum site temperature (5 degC)
    shall not exceed 1500 V.
5.  The inverter shall be a 4000 kVA central inverter with an MPPT window of
    875 V to 1325 V.
6.  The DC/AC ratio shall be between 1.20 and 1.30.
7.  The ground coverage ratio (GCR) shall not exceed 0.45 to limit inter-row
    shading losses.
8.  The row-to-row pitch shall be at least 6.0 m.
9.  The LT cable voltage drop shall not exceed 2.0 %.
10. The plant shall achieve a specific yield of at least 1550 kWh/kWp per year
    (site GHI approx 5.6 kWh/m2/day).
11. The available site area for this block is 60000 m2.
